# Swift Response Battalion

## Overview

The Swift Response Battalion is a paramilitary force formerly operated by the MSA. They originated as an auxiliary force militia used by the MSA during a series of military actions in the region, which later restructured as a paramilitary organization in 2023.

In 2032, the SRB briefly allied with the Ikwan-As-Sayf (IAS) to seize power in the region. As of 2034, the SRB have established it's own parallel government and no longer align with their former allies.
