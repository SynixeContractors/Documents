# The Covenant

## Overview

A doomsday cult that was established by "The Founder", Zachary DeWitt. The organization's belief system had been provided to Zachary by the divine visitation of an Angel. They believe that the world should give up its control to all-powerful computer systems and that humanity should be subsequently extinguished by the use of weapons of mass destruction. The original group based in Virelia has gained worldwide attention through its presence on social media. 

Zachary was sentenced to life in prison for making mustard gas in his garage, following a leak that led to 3 dead and over 400 injured.

Many years later, copycat chapters appear in many countries to this day. They set up training camps in remote areas, training until they are ready to strike following the founder's strategy. These attacks often lead to human casualties by storming military bases, civilian government buildings or chemical facilities, using both commercially procured weapons and improvised explosives.
