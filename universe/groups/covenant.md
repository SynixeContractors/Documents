# The Covenant

## Overview

A doomsday cult, established by "The Founder", Zachary DeWitt. The organization's belief system has been provided to Zachary by divine visitation of an Angel. They believe that the world should give up its control to all powerful computer systems and for humanity to be subsequently extinguished by use of weapons of mass destruction. The original group based in Virelia has gained worldwide attention by its presence on social media. 

Zachary has been sentenced to life in prison for making mustard gas in his garage, following a leak, leading to 3 dead and over 400 injured.

Many years later, copycat chapters appear in many countries to this day. They setup training camps in remote areas, training until they are ready to strike, following the founder's strategy. Often ultimately leading to human casualties by storming military bases, civilian government buildings or chemical facilities, using both commercially procured weapons, as well as improvised explosives.
