# Republic of Altis

## Overview

The Republic's origins are traced back to the ancient era, with the islands strategic position, they were ruled by vast merchant republics. The shores of Altis forming safe and calm bay made Pyrgos a prime destination of the acient era. Later in its life, the islands have come under imperial colonist rule. While the port facilities were used by the imperial fleet majority of the island has become impoverished.

While decolonized without spilling of blood, the islands situation in the world grew less important. In 2026 Col. Georgious Akhanteros carried out coup d'état in the new capital of Kavala. Acting upon pretense of removing the corrupt civilian leaders, who he claims, led to the downfall of the importance of the republic. After 5 years of bloody civil war with the Junta managing to state in power the economic situation has greatly improved after finding undersea oil deposits in its territorial waters. Old government loyalist to this day offer resistance that flares up every couple of years.
