# Krasnovian Republic

## Overview

The Republic is named after the vast taiga that was usually beyond the edges of the early maps reserved for barbarians. Nowadays its economy is based on resource extraction from that taiga and their excellent woodworking and furniture industry. With its economy relying on argricultural imports from the south, protection of its national interests has become vital in nation building.

They maintain large standing ground army mostly compromised of conscripts and largely reliant on motorized forces aswell as contract soldiers that specialize in arctic warfare. They are often parts of multinational military excercises where they teach others their craft in exchange for hard currency.
