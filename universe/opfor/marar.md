# Republic of Marar

## Overview

Marar is a very rich, small parliamentary republic located on the plateaus overlooking the historic Tura region. The country was previously ruled by the Irthysh family, whose ancestry goes back to the Sunset Khanate, but their rule came to an end 15 years ago amid a sexual abuse scandal involving the Crown Princess Tanuke. Following massive street protests, the nearly 800-year rule concluded in just two months. Great Khan Kerei, who had been overwhelmingly popular during his 44-year reign, ceremoniously abdicated the day before the parliamentary vote that would enshrine a new constitution and proclaim the First Republic of Marar. While the vote initially passed with only one abstention, the republic soon struggled to form a ruling coalition, cycling through 10 governments in three years. This instability led to nearly 15% of the vote going to the newly formed conservative Royal Restoration party.

In the following years, alongside a growing autonomy movement within the Tura minority, the Royal party's militia began cracking down on dissent, often working alongside the regular army and police. The exact significance of this development has not yet been realized.

The pride of the nation, the Royal Guards mechanized regiment, has often been involved in international peacekeeping missions on behalf of the UN, but it has also participated in brutal crackdowns against the Tura population.
